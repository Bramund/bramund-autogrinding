Which parts can the GV 270 fettle?

The GV 270 is designed for iron, steel, aluminum and other castings that fit within a diameter of up to 700 mm and a height of up to 400 mm, with a total weight of up to 90 kg including the fixture.

Key advantages

  • Easy programming: point-based programming with an interface designed specifically for fettling. Programs are created quickly, intuitively and efficiently.
  • Simple, low-cost fixtures: they work as a cradle for the casting. Easy to build, affordable and practical day to day.
  • Fast setup: changing the fixture and loading a new program takes less than 15 minutes, so automation pays off even for small batches.
  • Pre-machining: thanks to the high precision and consistency of CNC, pre-machining operations can be done on many parts, saving cost and later steps.
  • Laser measurement: the laser system detects dimensional variations in each part and prevents collisions, for a safe and reliable process.
  • High-performance diamond discs: developed for fettling, with strong removal power, exceptional durability and better production performance.
